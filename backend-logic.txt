==============backend endpoints==================
=================================================

authorization endpoints:
POST /auth - require username, password in body
POST /refresh

userHandler endpoints:
POST /api/user/create - require username(>3 && <= 40), password(>=8 && <= 100)
GET /api/user/profile - current authorized user. require authorization headers(Bearer accessToken)
GET /api/user/profile/{username} - any user. require username - pathValue
